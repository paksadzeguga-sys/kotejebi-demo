    Five shapes, roofing and its colour, site conditions.

  3. 3

    Facade

    Facade material and colour, windows, terrace, utilities.

  4. 4

    Layout

    Bedrooms, bathrooms and the arrangement.

  5. 5

    Interior

    Heating, hot water, sanitary ware, colours and additional works.

  6. Send

    Your selection reaches us on WhatsApp — as text and as a link. Nothing is sent on its own.

VISIT

A visit to the site.

On site we assess the terrain, access, foundation type, orientation and the permit route; you receive an architectural visualisation and a cost calculation.

  • Terrain
  • Access
  • Foundation type
  • Orientation
  • Route to the permit
  • You tick the visit on the configurator's last step and choose the plot's municipality — the price shows at once; we agree the date on WhatsApp.
  • The visit's price depends on the distance from Tbilisi: up to 400 km — 500–⁠2,000 ₾, farther — by agreement.
  • You pay for the visit in advance, and it is deducted from the contract price.
