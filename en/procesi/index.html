HOW WE BUILD

10 stages to a home

Ten stages in one order, from setting out to the terrace. Each stage is checked by photo before the next layer covers it.

THIS IS HOW WE BUILD

  1. 01Setting out, postsPosts no more than 2 m apart.
  2. 02Floor structureJoists, lower and upper sheet.
  3. 03Wall frame and diagonalsStuds 5/⁠15 at 60 cm; a diagonal on every wall.
  4. 04Roof frameRafters, ridge, gables.
  5. 05Membrane and battensWind membrane; a 25 mm air gap on battens.
  6. 06Roof covering and drainageMetal sheet on membrane and battens; gutter and downpipe.
  7. 07FacadeFacade board 13 mm, a ventilated facade. The oil is renewed every 4 years.
  8. 08OpeningsWindows and the entrance door; a header above each opening.
  9. 09Insulation and vapour barrierStone wool 3 × 50 = 150 mm between studs, 150 mm in the ceiling; a vapour membrane on the warm side.
  10. 10Interior panel, floor, utility zone, terraceThe wall: six layers, 202 mm.
