WHO IT IS FOR

Is your house being built? Are you a craftsman?

Both sides get one standard: ten stages, a daily plan and one photo at every stage.

01

Your house is being built

By any builder — us or another. First you check the offer, then the build, stage by stage.

  • Questions for the builder — answers in writing, in the contract.
  • An indicative term next to the builder's promise.
  • A calendar by days and a photo at every stage — once switched on.

TEN STAGES

One photo at every stage.

The photo is taken before the layer is covered — after that nobody can see it again. At every stage: what to shoot, what to check and by when.

  1. 01

    Setting out, posts

    SHOOT
    The row of posts from the side — a tape measure visible between two posts.
    CHECK
    No span over 2.0 m anywhere; the space under the floor is open.
    LAST CHANCE
    before the bottom floor sheet is closed
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 4
    1. Clearing and setting out the plot — outline, checking the diagonals
    2. Post positions, preparing the footings
    3. Setting the square-tube posts, levelling them
    4. Bottom beam on the posts — bearing lines, checking the span
  2. 02

    Floor structure

    SHOOT
    The joists from above, stone wool in place — before the top sheet closes it.
    CHECK
    60 cm between joists, section 5/⁠15; stone wool up to the joist height, no gaps.
    LAST CHANCE
    before the OSB 18 is closed
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 4
    1. Floor joists 50×150, 60 cm spacing — laying out and fixing
    2. Bottom sheet OSB 9 from below
    3. Stone wool 150 mm between the joists — three layers
    4. Top sheet OSB 18 — staggered joints
  3. 03

    Wall frame and diagonals

    SHOOT
    The wall from outside — three studs, the diagonal and the blocking in the frame.
    CHECK
    60 cm spacing, studs 5/⁠15, a diagonal on every wall.
    LAST CHANCE
    before the membrane covers it
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 5
    1. Wall frames laid flat — studs 5/⁠15, 60 cm spacing (walls 1–⁠2)
    2. Wall frames — walls 3–⁠4, headers over the openings
    3. Raising, temporary braces, level and plumb
    4. Diagonal bracing on every wall
    5. Horizontal blocking between the studs, top plate
  4. 04

    Roof frame

    SHOOT
    The rafters from below — spacing and section visible.
    CHECK
    Even spacing, the same section — both are stated in the design.
    LAST CHANCE
    before the membrane
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 3
    1. Making and laying out the rafters
    2. Fixing the rafters, the ridge
    3. Overhangs and gables
  5. 05

    Membrane and battens

    SHOOT
    The bottom edge of the facade up close — membrane, batten, open gap.
    CHECK
    Membrane under the battens, joints overlapped; 25 mm gap, bottom edge open.
    LAST CHANCE
    before the facade panel
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 3
    1. Wind membrane on the walls — bottom to top, overlapping joints
    2. Battens on the walls — 25 mm air gap, mesh at the bottom edge
    3. Membrane and battens on the roof
  6. 06

    Roof covering and drainage

    SHOOT
    The roof edge — metal sheet, screws, gutter and downpipe.
    CHECK
    Metal 0.5 mm on the invoice; screws with rubber washers; the gutter falls towards the downpipe.
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 3
    1. Metal sheet 0.5 mm — screws with rubber washers
    2. Ridge piece, wind boards, flashings
    3. Gutter and downpipe
  7. 07

    Facade

    SHOOT
    A facade corner — the panel on a batten.
    CHECK
    The panel is on battens, not on the membrane; the bottom edge is open (mesh).
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 3
    1. Facade panel 13 mm on the battens — walls 1–⁠2
    2. Facade panel — walls 3–⁠4, corner trims
    3. Treating the facade with oil
  8. 08

    Openings

    SHOOT
    A window opening from inside — header on top, foam and tape.
    CHECK
    A header above the opening; foam covered with tape; membranes folded into the opening.
    LAST CHANCE
    before the interior lining
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 3
    1. Fitting the windows — foam and tape
    2. Entrance door
    3. Sealing the openings, sills
  9. 09

    Insulation and barrier

    SHOOT
    First the pipes and cables in the frame — before covering; then three layers of stone wool; then the inner membrane with tape.
    CHECK
    Stone wool 15 cm, no gaps, to the full stud depth; membrane on the warm side, joints taped.
    LAST CHANCE
    before the interior panel
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 4
    1. Electrical and water lines in the frame — to their points, photo before covering
    2. Stone wool in the walls — three layers × 50 mm
    3. Stone wool in the ceiling — 150 mm
    4. Vapour membrane on the inside, joints taped
  10. 10

    Interior panel, floor, tech. zone, terrace

    SHOOT
    The edge of the panel — the membrane visible underneath; the terrace posts; the technical zone's door and ventilation.
    CHECK
    The membrane shows at the panel edge; the decking is clear of the ground; the annex is ventilated.
    LAST CHANCE
    before handover
    WORK AT THIS STAGE· 8
    1. Interior timber panel — ceiling
    2. Interior timber panel — walls
    3. Wet area — board, waterproofing, tiles
    4. Floor — underlay 3 mm, laminate 10 mm, skirting
    5. Electrical panel, sockets, switches; plumbing points
    6. Technical zone — annex or cabinet, place for the water heater
    7. Terrace — posts, decking, railing, steps
    8. Interior doors, cleaning, snag list, handover

SWITCHING ON

What is free, what needs switching on.

The passport, the questions, the indicative term and the craftsman's part are free. The calendar and the photo check are switched on — once, for the whole build.

FREE

  • The wall passport and the “ask the builder” list.
  • The indicative term — next to the builder's promise.
  • “Already built” — 8 points you can still see.

  • “What to do” — for every doubtful photo.
  • The schedule sheet for the builder.

FEE

1% of the budget

Once. Valid to the end of the plan and 30 more days. The build runs longer than planned? We extend the term without a new fee.

HOW IT IS SWITCHED ON

  1. 01Enter the site and the budget in the app — the fee (1%) shows right there.
  2. 02“Switch on Control” opens WhatsApp: the site details and the fee go to Kotejebi.
  3. 03After payment Kotejebi sends you an activation code.
  4. 04Enter the code in the app. The server checks it — the calendar and the photo check open.

Separately: engineer check of a photo · engineer visit to the site.

Terms: The app helps you check; you make the decisions. The builder answers for the build — under the contract. Photos stay on this device. The site's location leaves it only in a message you send — you see it before you send.

TERMS

One thing, one name.

On site the same layer goes by different names. Here: the term and what the part does.

Header
A beam above an opening — it takes the frame's load off the window.
Vapour membrane
The membrane on the inside of the stone wool — it keeps room vapour out of the wall.
Wind membrane
A breathable membrane on the outside of the stone wool — stops wind and damp, lets vapour out.
Air gap
25 mm between the facade and the wall — the facade dries from behind.
Battens
Thin boards the facade or the metal sheet is fixed to — they make the air gap.
Batten
A thin strip of timber.
Stud
A vertical member of the frame, 5/⁠15, at 60 cm spacing.
Rafter
A sloping beam of the roof — it carries the roof's load to the walls.
Diagonal
A sloping board in the frame — it braces the wall against racking.
Stone wool
Insulation made of stone fibre — 50 kg/⁠m³, three layers × 5 cm.
Metal sheet
Coloured metal roofing, 0.5 mm — the roof's first shield.
Screw
Self-tapping — with a rubber washer on the roof.
Underlay
A thin layer under the laminate, 3 mm.
Gutter
The channel at the roof edge — it leads the water to the downpipe.
Post
The support that lifts the cottage off the ground; span up to 2 m.
Joist
A horizontal load-bearing timber — floor joist 50×150 at 60 cm spacing.
Membrane
A thin layer on the stone wool — against wind outside, against vapour inside.
Canopy
A roofed open space on posts.
