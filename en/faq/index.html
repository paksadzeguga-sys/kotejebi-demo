Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What is included in the price?

    The price includes:

    • Foundation
    • Structure and frame
    • Roof
    • Doors and windows
    • Utilities — electricity, water, drainage run to their points
    • Plumbing — toilet, basin, shower (standard set)
    • Interior finish — floors, walls, ceilings

    Not in the price / added separately:

    • Permit and design — 8,000 ₾ (a dwelling, class II); the municipal fee is separate
    • Site visit — 500–⁠2,000 ₾ by distance; paid in advance and deducted from the contract price
    • By your choice: Heating, Hot water, Septic tank, Well, Outside connection to the networks — water, sewage, power, Terrace — open $250, covered $350 per m² of terrace
    • Furniture and household appliances

    The exact price is agreed.

  2. Price per square meter?

    Standard package from $550/⁠m², premium with aluminium windows $650/⁠m². The configurator calculates an indicative price; the exact price is agreed.

  3. How long does a price hold?

    A price from the configurator or the price tool holds for 5 calendar days from the day it was filled in. It is indicative: the exact price is agreed.

  4. What works are completed at this price?

    1. Setting out, posts
    2. Floor structure
    3. Wall frame and diagonals
    4. Roof frame
    5. Membrane and battens
    6. Roof covering and drainage
    7. Facade
    8. Openings
    9. Insulation and vapour barrier
    10. Interior panel, floor, utility zone, terrace

    You get the cottage turnkey: only furniture and appliances are yours to bring in.

  5. In what time do you submit the project?

    A cottage takes 2–⁠3 months; a house from 130 m², 3–⁠6 months.

  6. What does the permit cost, and how long does it take?

    The design and the permit documents are a design service: 8,000 ₾ for a house or cottage (class II), 4,000 ₾ for a class I building (non-residential, up to 50 m²). The municipal fee is separate.

    Getting the permit takes about 1 month for class I and 3–⁠4 months for class II. It can take longer: the municipality sets the pace.

  7. How many sites do you run at once?

    We have run up to 10 sites at the same time. The sites work every day, Sundays too; the office rests on Sundays.

  8. Do you build in winter?

    Yes, we build in winter too, and it is a good time for the wood. In the cold, mould and wood-decay fungi barely grow and insects are inactive, while the timber dries slowly and evenly and cracks less than under the hot summer sun. Before the walls are closed we clear snow and ice so the frame stays dry.

  9. do you build in the regions?

    We build all over Georgia — in the mountains and by the sea alike. Where we have built is on the works page.

    The site visit is priced by distance: 500–⁠2,000 ₾, up to 400 km from Tbilisi; paid in advance and deducted from the contract price.

  10. Do you build on a slope?

    Yes. A timber cottage stands on posts: a square steel tube set in a pit and poured with concrete, the posts up to 2 m apart. The house is raised off the ground and aired underneath — so it stands on a sloping plot too.

    The slope changes the price: the configurator gives an indicative figure; the ground and the site visit decide the final foundation.

  11. I live abroad — can I build?

    Yes. Many customers living abroad have built their houses with us remotely. The details are agreed on a call.

  12. Do you only build wooden cottages?

    Our company can offer you not only the construction of a wooden cottage, but also the construction of concrete and iron structures.

  13. Do you build to my own or a changed design?

    A catalogue design is the starting point: you choose the area, shape, rooms, facade, windows and terrace in the configurator, and the price shows at once. The design service prepares the design and the permit documents.

    Your own design we go through on a call and agree the details.

  14. terms of payment

    The sum is paid in three parts:

    • 50% — when the contract is signed
    • 40% — once the roof is covered — stage 6, “Roof covering and drainage”
    • 10% — at the handover of the finished house

  15. What material do you use?

    We mainly use Russian / Belarusian pine, we use larch material for the terrace floor.

  16. How are the wall and the ceiling insulated?

    In the wall, stone wool fills the space between the studs: 3 × 50 = 150 mm at 50 kg/⁠m³. A wind membrane is outside; on the warm side inside, a vapour membrane keeps the room's vapour out of the wool. Behind the facade panel is a 25 mm air gap — the ventilated facade dries from behind. The ceiling has 150 mm of stone wool.

    The wall is six layers, 202 mm in all — layer by layer on the materials page.

  17. What does the fire risk of a timber house depend on?

    The fire risk depends on proper treatment and care. The insulation is stone wool: 150 mm in the wall, 150 mm in the ceiling.

  18. How do you allow for earthquakes?

    The frame gives the strength: studs 60 cm apart, two diagonals on every wall, one at each end, sloping opposite ways. The posts are steel tube set in concrete, up to 2 m apart.

  19. How is the timber kept from damp and rot?

    The timber does not touch the ground: the house stands on steel posts, raised and aired underneath. Behind the facade panel is a 25 mm air gap — the facade dries from behind. A wind membrane is outside; on the warm side inside, a vapour membrane keeps the room's vapour out of the wool. Under the roof's metal sheet are a membrane and battens. The facade lacquer is renewed every 4 years.

  20. What is the warranty, and from when does it count?

    Warranty: structure 5 years, utilities 2 years, finishing 1 year. The warranty counts from the day the house is handed over.

  21. Does the facade need care?

    Yes: the facade's clear oil-based lacquer is renewed every 4 years. The first coat is laid and checked at the facade stage (stage 7).

  22. Do you bring a ready-made cottage?

    No, all materials are coming to the site and construction work will be done on place.
